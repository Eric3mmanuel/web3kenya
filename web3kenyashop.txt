Ahhh — **now we're talking.** 😎

I wouldn't build Web3Kenya's shop as a generic “crypto store.” That would put you in competition with every electronics shop selling Ledger, smartwatches and gaming accessories.

I'd build it as **the physical lifestyle layer of Web3Kenya**.

The question becomes:

> **“What does someone who lives, works, plays and identifies with Web3 actually want to own?”**

Looking at the current ecosystem, there's already a market for hardware wallets and physical security gear in Kenya, while Web3-native stores are increasingly positioning products around **self-custody, onchain identity, node gear and lifestyle**, rather than simply “crypto gadgets.” ([Vivid Gold - Video Game Outlet [KE]][1])

**“Protect what you own.”**
