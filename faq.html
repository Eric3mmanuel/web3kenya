Web3 is a newer version of the internet that uses blockchain technology to give people more control over their digital assets, identity and online interactions. It includes technologies such as cryptocurrencies, smart contracts, digital wallets and decentralized applications (dApps).

Blockchain is a technology that stores information and transactions on a shared digital network instead of relying on one central database. Because records can be verified across the network, blockchain can make digital transactions more transparent, secure and difficult to alter.

Web3 in Kenya refers to the growing use of blockchain, digital assets, smart contracts, Web3 applications and decentralized technologies in the Kenyan market. It creates opportunities for businesses, developers, creators and individuals to build and use new types of digital services.

Businesses in Kenya can use Web3 for digital payments, blockchain-based applications, customer loyalty programs, digital assets, smart contracts, identity solutions and other blockchain-powered services. The right use case depends on the business and the problem it wants to solve.
